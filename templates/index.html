⚽ Football Club Logo Game

Can you guess the club from the blurred logo?

How to Play

  • You'll see 10 random football club logos
  • Each logo starts heavily blurred
  • You have 5 attempts to guess each club
  • After each wrong guess, the blur decreases
  • Score points based on how fast you guess!

Scoring:

1st attempt: 10 points | 2nd: 8 points | 3rd: 7 points

4th attempt: 6 points | 5th: 5 points
